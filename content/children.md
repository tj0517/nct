# Lessons for Children

A word can be small.
A word can be grand.
A word can lead to
a faraway land.

At NCT, children do not just learn English.
They explore it.

- Stories.
- Questions.
- Rhymes.
- Conversations.
- Books with strange doors.
- Sentences with wings.

We help children speak clearly,
write boldly,
and use English with confidence.

English is not just a subject at school.

It is a key,
a ladder,
and a door you pass through.
